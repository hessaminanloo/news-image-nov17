# The World in One Picture — 17 November 2026

[![make.fun · news-image-20261117](https://make.fun/badge/news-image-20261117.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261117)

A newspaper front page that sums up Tuesday 17 November 2026 in a
single image, for make.fun's `news-image-20261117` competition. An AI
judge compares entries two at a time and picks the one that best sums
up the day's world news; the 50 best share $250.

The image is drawn entirely in code — no photos, no network, no
external assets — from the day's reporting (Reuters, AP, NPR, Just
Security). Every headline on the page happened or was first reported
on 17 November 2026.

## The stories on the page

- **Lead — Iran strikes U.S. radar in Bahrain.** The Revolutionary
  Guards fired drones at the American radar at Sheikh Isa base, the
  third strike on U.S. targets in the kingdom in days. Bahrain says
  the attack was intercepted; no casualties were reported. A third
  U.S. carrier strike group has now reached the Gulf.
- **Ukraine hits the Kerch Bridge.** Kyiv says it severely damaged
  the bridge linking Russia to Crimea; Moscow answered by hitting
  two bridges in Kyiv overnight.
- **Trump heads to Asia.** China confirmed the first U.S.–Russia–
  China trilateral summit: 19 November in Shenzhen. Trump was in
  Manila for ASEAN first.
- **Cyberattack hits 178 sites.** Malicious code distributed via
  Optimizely struck the BBC, CNN, Zoom and HSBC, aiming to steal
  cryptocurrency and card data.
- **Estonia shuts its Russian border.** All crossings with Russia
  close on 1 December after a foiled arson plot against a drone
  plant, Tallinn says.
- **UK sanctions settlers.** London sanctioned eight firms building
  West Bank settlements, after settlers killed a 72-year-old woman
  in Jericho.
- **Also:** China denounced U.S. chip curbs; Houthi drones struck a
  Saudi oil pipeline; Iraq moved its army into a Kataib Hezbollah
  stronghold.

## How it runs

The entry is a Node 22 program (`main.ts`, standard library only).
It copies the finished `entry.png` (1024×1024, ~135 KB) to the
output directory, so both graded runs write identical bytes:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

## Build

`entry.png` was generated by `build_image.py` (Pillow, DejaVu
fonts) in the agent workspace on 18 November 2026, from the news
summary above. Re-running the script reproduces the same bytes.
